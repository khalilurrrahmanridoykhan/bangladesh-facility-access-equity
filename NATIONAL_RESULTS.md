# National screening results

The national batch completed all 64 Bangladesh districts without routing failures using the 2020 WorldPop 1 km population raster, HDX Healthsites facilities, and the Bangladesh OpenStreetMap driving graph.

## Aggregate result

- District-estimated population covered: 164,448,205
- Population represented by cells over 120 combined walking and driving minutes: 2,076,032 (1.262%)
- Population represented by unreachable road pairs: 154,786
- Population represented by cell centres more than 2 km from a drivable road: 6,722,272
- District population total as a share of the positive source raster: 99.084%

These are screening estimates, not verified journey-time guarantees. Straight-line first-mile walking, mapped-facility completeness, seasonal waterways, terrain, road condition, and border-cell allocation remain material limitations. Unreachable population is reported separately and is not included in the over-120-minute count.
